Most founders wait too long to get help with their calendar. Here are five signs it's already costing you.

**1. You're the one moving your own meetings.** If you're regularly the person resolving scheduling conflicts, sending reschedule emails, or hunting for a mutual time, that's hours a week that shouldn't require your judgment.

**2. Travel planning eats an evening.** Booking flights, comparing hotel options, and building an itinerary is a 45-minute task for someone who does it daily — and a two-hour task for someone who doesn't.

**3. Your inbox has become a to-do list.** When "check email" starts meaning "figure out what I'm supposed to be doing today," your inbox has taken over a role it was never meant to play.

**4. Board prep happens the night before.** If board decks, data rooms, and investor updates are coming together at the last minute, it's usually not a content problem — it's a coordination problem.

**5. You've said "I'll just do it myself" more than once this month.** It's often faster in the moment. It's rarely faster over a quarter.

None of these require a full-time hire to fix. They require consistent, senior-level support for the hours where it actually matters — which is exactly what a fractional executive assistant is built for.
